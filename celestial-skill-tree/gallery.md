# Gallery

Title and description for each picture in `images/gallery`, ready to paste into the CurseForge and Modrinth galleries.

## 01-star-chart.png

**Title:** The Star Chart

**Description:** Every class is a constellation and every skill is a star. The whole tree is drawn from your world's
seed, so no two worlds share the same sky.

## 02-keystone-juggernaut.png

**Title:** Keystones

**Description:** Keystones sit at the tip of each class and change how you play: a big advantage that always comes
with a drawback.

## 03-keystone-thousand-cuts.png

**Title:** Plan before you spend

**Description:** Mark a whole route and see its price before buying. Nothing is applied until you press Confirm, and
you can undo or redo along the way.

## 04-black-hole.png

**Title:** Black Holes

**Description:** A black hole makes the skills you own around it stronger, but seals the ones you don't. Choose
carefully where you let gravity in.

## 05-astral-gate.png

**Title:** Astral Gates

**Description:** Gates link opposite sides of the chart. Learn one and its twin opens on the far side, so you can
jump across the sky.

## 06-stats-panel.png

**Title:** Character Stats

**Description:** Your character sheet: every attribute the tree changes, a preview of your pending selection, and
your conditional bonuses, triggered effects and keystones added up.

## 07-birth-chart.png

**Title:** Birth Chart

**Description:** On your first login the sky picks one of twelve birth signs. Its small bonus is permanent and stays
with you through every reset.

## 08-eclipse.png

**Title:** Eclipse

**Description:** Some days the moon covers the sun. Monsters walk in daylight and the undead don't burn, but respecs
are free until the eclipse ends.

## 09-legendary-tabs.png

**Title:** Legendary Tabs

**Description:** With Legendary Tabs installed, a Celestial Skill Tree tab in your inventory opens the star chart.

## 10-jei-atlas.png

**Title:** JEI Pages

**Description:** With JEI installed, every item and block of the mod has an information page saying where it comes
from and what it does.
